Who we are

Next-Generation Engineering eXcellence Organization

Rooted in Baghdad, NEXO closes the gap between the model and the site. We bring BIM-grade technical modelling, advanced construction chemistry, and local workforce development under one practice — so what gets drawn is what gets built.

Vision
To be Iraq's leading multidisciplinary engineering practice, setting the benchmark for architectural modelling, structural durability, and technical education.
Mission
Deliver turnkey civil contracting, architectural precision, and practical software training by pairing international engineering standards with strategic industrial partnerships.
Where we work
Commercial, hospitality, petroleum, and urban development projects across Iraq — from concept package to handover.
